Token options

The redesign’s styling tokens (#646), as approved after eight rounds & applied to the app. Every section shows the settled choice; 2K stays beside 2M as design history. The strip above switches the whole sheet between light & 1D. The sprites are stand-ins drawn for this sheet.

1What dark mode is made of

Settled: 1D, the faded cyanotype.

2Thumb tab colors

Ten tabs, ten everyday color names, white letters on every light tab, and a text token per category, with Mines in stone gray-brown. 2M tunes round six’s 2K: a quieter Recipes in the light theme, and a darker Mines tab in dark mode. The figures under each set are the closest pair’s distance (OKLab ΔE), for normal vision & the two common color-vision deficiencies; bigger means easier to tell apart.

3The Not now sprite

Settled: 3A+.

4Who made the mark

Settled: four tools, in two of the palette’s own colors. Done is the green and obtained is the gold, so the marks add no colors of their own.

5One meter, three directions

Renown is a climbing gold star (5F): it rides the top of a gold bar and names your town rank. The star is our own plump drawing, after the game’s rank stars.

6Type roles, snapped to the scale

The mockup’s half-pixel sizes round to the nearest step, favoring the larger. The body face is Petrona, as today. The hand face is a stand-in until #677 picks one.
