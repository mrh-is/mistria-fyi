Round six of the redesign’s styling tokens (#646): everything here is settled except Mines’ color and the Renown meter. The strip above switches the whole sheet between light & 1D. The sprites are stand-ins drawn for this sheet.

1What dark mode is made of

Settled: 1D, the faded cyanotype.

2Thumb tab colors

Ten tabs, ten everyday color names, white letters on every light tab, and a text token per category. The open question is Mines: 2G keeps it brown; 2J makes it slate gray and 2K stone gray-brown, each refitted so the warm colors could spread out. Normal vision barely notices the difference. Without full color vision, both grays do better, mostly in dark mode. The figures under each set are the closest pair’s distance (OKLab ΔE), for normal vision & the two common color-vision deficiencies; bigger means easier to tell apart.

4Who made the mark

Settled: four tools, in two of the palette’s own colors. Done is the green and obtained is the gold, so the marks add no colors of their own.

5One meter, three directions

Renown has lost its grass, since it’s no longer a flagpole. The star is our own plump drawing, after the game’s rank stars, and the rank colors come from those stars, made readable on each paper. 5B is the settled meter; 5C & 5D bring in the town ranks, which change every 10 levels.
