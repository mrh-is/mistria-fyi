Token options

Round three of the redesign’s styling tokens (#646). Settled since round two: 3A+ for Not now, colored provenance marks, 5B’s drawn meters, & Petrona for the body. The strip above reprints the whole sheet on any paper, and every “dark” panel below follows the last dark paper you picked. The sprites are stand-ins drawn for this sheet.

1What dark mode is made of

You lived in 1D through round two. 1F is new: 1E’s blue-black with the lamp turned down, so the paper is a step darker and the ink is a soft gray instead of near-white. Its body text is still 11:1 against the paper. Night slate stays as the safe reference; the cyanotype & petrol are gone.

2Thumb tab colors

Every letter on a strip now takes the same ink. Each category has one hue, read off the game (the key below the sets), in two strengths: a deep one that holds white letters and reads as text on light paper, and a pale one that holds dark letters and reads as text on dark paper. 2E uses deep tabs in the light theme; 2F uses pale tabs in both, like the game’s own menu. 2C stays for comparison. The figures under each set are the closest pair’s distance (OKLab ΔE), for normal vision & the two common color-vision deficiencies; bigger means easier to tell apart.

3The Not now sprite

Settled: 3A+. Round one’s 3A stays beside it for reference. Every page above already uses 3A+.

4Who made the mark

Four tools, green & amber. Round two’s amber drifted yellower in the dark theme; both pairs here keep one hue across light & dark. 4A is the record’s pair with the drift fixed. 4C turns the chroma up, so checking things off feels like more of an event.

5One meter, three directions

5B, cleaned up. Mines & Renown now share one ground line: Renown rises above it and Mines is cut below it, so the direction shows before there’s any progress. The grass is drawn as tufts instead of a sawtooth, the shaft & pole are the same length, and the counts sit on the same side. Skills keeps the plain ruler.

6Type roles, snapped to the scale

The mockup’s half-pixel sizes round to the nearest step, favoring the larger. The body face is Petrona, as today. The hand face is a stand-in until #677 picks one.
