Token options

Round two of the redesign’s styling tokens (#646), after your first read. The strip above reprints the whole sheet on any paper, and every “dark” panel below follows the last dark paper you picked. The sprites are stand-ins drawn for this sheet.

1What dark mode is made of

Pencil doesn’t read on dark paper, so dark mode names a material. The papers below run from the cyanotype down to night slate, with two new steps between them. 1D keeps about three quarters of the cyanotype’s blue, and 1E about half. Pick one in the strip and read the rest of the sheet on it for a while.

2Thumb tab colors

2C is new: hues keyed to the game’s menu, “blue for blue”, at a strength between the jewel tones & the dyed cloth. 2D is the same set at full strength, to bracket it. Each set is drawn on the light cover & the dark one, then as text on the page. The figures under each set are the closest pair’s distance (OKLab ΔE), for normal vision & the two common color-vision deficiencies; bigger means easier to tell apart.

3The Not now sprite

You picked 3A, washed out, and asked for more. Two paler steps follow it. The palest still keeps the drawing’s inner lines, which is what keeps it apart from the spoiler silhouette.

4Who made the mark

The four tools stay. They’re a settled decision (“one X, four tools”, from October), and none of its reasons to revisit has come up. The open question is narrower: the record says done is green & obtained is amber, while the round-ten mockup draws everything in graphite.

5One meter, three directions

Round one’s ruler spent its ink on ticks, and an empty Mines meter looked the same as an empty Renown one. Both options here make the fill the loudest thing, keep only the labeled ticks, and anchor each meter at its start so the direction shows before there’s any progress. Each Mines & Renown meter is drawn empty, then partway.

6Type roles, snapped to the scale

The mockup’s half-pixel sizes round to the nearest step, favoring the larger. The hand face is a stand-in until #677 picks one.
