Token options

A first pass at the redesign’s styling tokens (#646). The light palette follows round ten. Four things are open, and each section below shows its options side by side. The sprites here are stand-ins drawn for this sheet.

1What dark mode is made of

Pencil doesn’t read on dark paper, so dark mode has to name a material. Every panel draws the same page: hand digits, a pencil X, the Later hatching, the three sprite states, & a taped note. The branch implements 1A.

2Thumb tab colors

Each set is shown on the light cover & the dark one. The second row is the same color used as text on the page. The branch keeps 2A in light and lightens it for the cyanotype. I couldn’t open the game’s menu art from this checkout, so neither set is checked hue-for-hue against the game.

3The Not now sprite

To do prints the sprite in one ink, and Completed keeps the game’s colors, like a sticker. Not now needs to look “out of reach today” without looking like the spoiler silhouette. All of these are CSS filters & masks on one image. The branch implements 3A.

4Who made the mark

Today the tool says who made a mark (#276): a pen is you, a rubber stamp is the save, pencil is inferred, & a typewriter is unknown. The mockup draws everything in pencil. The branch defines only the pencil until you choose.

5One meter, three directions

Nothing to decide here unless it looks wrong. Skills, Mines, & Renown share one ruler: a printed rule & ticks, with the player’s level drawn along it in pencil. Deeper goes down, and a higher rank goes up.

6Type roles, snapped to the scale

The mockup’s half-pixel sizes round to the nearest step, favoring the larger. The hand face is a stand-in until #677 picks one.
