Mistria Field Guide

Round ten again, after your notes: a cooler book, Home turned toward today, every page editable, and three real saves from day one to late game. The ledger's sticky head now names the shelf you're on, and a column head filters instead of highlighting.

Everything here comes from Dewey's saves, run through the app's own projections.

The book

  1. A cooler palette: slate paper, a petrol cloth cover, and cobalt where the guide speaks. Source Serif replaces Petrona for body text.
  2. Home has its own thumb tab, at the top of the stack. The tabs carry no names or numbers; hover one for its name. Home's left column holds the contents & their numbers, and the rest is today.
  3. Every number can be set by hand: click a heart, a skill ruler, the mines chart, or the renown thermometer, or use − and +.
  4. The half screen's title is the contents button. It names the section you've scrolled to, and each sticky section head opens the contents too.

Ledgers

  1. No “Show more”: every row renders.
  2. To do keeps the “Later” column for sources you haven't reached. On Not now, every source comes out into its column; rows that come back by waiting are listed first, alphabetized, then the ones that need you to progress.
  3. Whole sections can be spoilered: quest types behind a villager or a renown level, and animals you haven't unlocked. Day one hides Missions, Crown quests, & Cooking challenges.
  4. Subsections are back: each animal's colors & cosmetics, Pets & the Mount split, and museum sets under their wing.
  5. Column heads name the detail: “Wing” on museum rewards, “Bundle” on scroll bundles, “Price” at the statues, and “Station” for anything with a recipe.

Data to fix

  1. Station names: the tracker has no workstation per recipe, so stations use the app's method names (“Craft”, “Cook”, “Smith”, “Mill”, “Dragon Forge”) plus the Apiary & Terrarium.
  2. Renown rewards list only items; the tracker data has no money rewards.
  3. Full Restore has no source in the data; the mockup grants it at floor 10, per the wiki.
