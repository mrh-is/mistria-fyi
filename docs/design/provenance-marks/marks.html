Provenance marks

Each checked box says who made the mark by what made it. The player inks it, the save stamps it, we pencil it in, and the old records were typed. Ink & pencil are hands, so they wander and can break the box; the stamp & the typewriter are machines, so they land square inside it.

Variants

Two or three per source. The one marked “pick” is the recommendation. Every pick differs from the other three in shape, and none reuses the lone slash that already means “obtained”.

On the page

Recommended marks on tracker rows, transcribed from tracker-row.svelte. Row text & struck-through state are unchanged; only the mark varies.

No legend on the page. The marks are texture, not a status system. A player who wonders learns the vocabulary once, from the description or a line in the help, and then reads it at a glance.

Obtained keeps its slash. The amber single stroke (Active quests, here) is unchanged and collides with none of the four.
